What if Manchester City lose points?

The Premier League has confirmed that an independent commission found Manchester City guilty of all charges of breaking its financial rules between 2009/10 and 2017/18. Sanctions will be decided at a separate hearing. Choose a points deduction for any season below. The final table is re-ranked straight away, and the page shows who would gain or lose the title, European places and survival.

How this works

Data

  • Final tables for 2009/10 to 2025/26 come from each season's Wikipedia article, parsed by build_data.py. Each table is checked for 38 games per club and an order that follows the ranking rules.
  • Historic deductions are kept: Portsmouth (−9, 2009/10), Everton (−8) and Nottingham Forest (−4) in 2023/24.
  • 2026/27 is under way and is not included.

Ranking and European places

  • Clubs are ranked on points, then goal difference, then goals scored. Head-to-head records are not modelled.
  • European places follow each season's own rules. Places earned through the FA Cup or League Cup pass down the table when the winner has already qualified. Fair play places and UEFA title holders keep their places. With no deduction, the model reproduces every actual qualifier and relegated club.

What is not modelled

  • Each season is re-ranked on its own. A club spared relegation would have played the next season in the Premier League, but later tables stay as they were.
  • England's fifth Champions League place in 2024/25 and 2025/26 depended on English clubs' results in Europe, including City's. It is treated as fixed.
  • Prize money, stripped trophies beyond the league table, and deductions for other clubs.
